How we research

How we pick what's in your plan

Every pick in your plan is checked from several angles before it makes the cut.

  • Value — Is it priced fairly for what it earns?
  • Growth — Is the market it serves growing?
  • Numbers — What does the data show about momentum and how much it swings?
  • Risk — What could go wrong? Debt, regulation, and fragile supply chains can flag or remove a pick.
  • Scenarios — We look at what happens if things go badly, as expected, or really well.
  • Freshness — Research updates twice a day from company filings, earnings, and headlines.
  • Honesty — No paid data, no copied articles, no guarantees. Educational research only — not personal advice.
