How the Odds calculator works

Players — Pick how many players are at the table, including you. Equity is against random hands for everyone else.

Your hand — Tap a slot to pick a card. Tap a filled card to remove it.

Board — Enter the flop first, then the turn, then the river. Tap a filled board card to remove it and any later board cards.

Pot odds (optional) — Fill in the pot before your call and the bet to call, and the advice compares your equity to the price the pot is offering.

Card picker — Cards already on the table are dimmed and can’t be picked twice.

What the numbers mean — Win / Tie / Lose are your chances against random hands. Outs are cards that improve your hand. Pot odds are the equity you need to break even on a call.

Limits — Equity is against random hands — real opponents play tighter, so treat it as a guide, not gospel. It doesn’t know your position, stack sizes, or how anyone plays. For learning and home games — check house or casino rules before using it live.
